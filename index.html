How it works

A clear route from idea to execution.

Every project is different. Our process gives the client a structured starting point while allowing the project scope to determine the appropriate next steps.

01 — DISCOVER

Consultation

Understand the vision, site, requirements, priorities and intended outcome.

02 — ASSESS

Project Review

Review available information and identify key technical or planning considerations.

03 — PLAN

Design & Scope

Shape the appropriate design, technical scope, budget direction and project pathway.

04 — EXECUTE

Construction

Coordinate the agreed project activities with attention to quality and communication.

05 — DELIVER

Handover

Review the completed scope and move the project toward its intended final use.
